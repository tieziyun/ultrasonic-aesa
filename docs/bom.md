## Optimizations made (2026-10-22)

- Transducer: Same Sky CUSA-TR65 (Ø14 mm, sealed, $3.55 at 100) -> CUSP-TR80-15-2500-TH (Ø9.8 mm, not sealed, 105 dB vs 100 dB SPL, 0.2-15 m, 1.6 ms ring-down, $1.65): about $122 less per board.
- ADC: 8 x AD7606C-16 ($58.81 each, $470 per board) → 16 x TI TLV320ADC5140 4-channel audio ADC ($4.01 each, about $64 per board). Same simultaneous sampling of all 64 channels, 192 kSPS, more dynamic range; needs a +3V3A LDO (second TPS7A4700) and the 3 A TPS54302 buck.
- SPH0641LU4H-1 is stocked by JLCPCB (C2879853), so it is assembled instead of consigned.
- +5VA LDO TPS7A4701RGWR (JLCPCB stock 1, $13.16) → TPS7A4700RGWT (646 in stock, $6.11): same chip family, pinout and ANY-OUT network.
- BAV99 → Nexperia BAV99,215 C2500, a Basic part (no $3 fee).
- Input protection: TPS26600 eFuse (60 V / -60 V, off above 13.8 V, 1.6 A, auto-retry) replaces the SS54 diode (0.5 W less heat); the polyfuse stays after it as a back-up.
- Polyfuse 1812L200/16DR → SMD1812P200TF16 (same 2 A hold / 16 V, $0.12 instead of $0.21).
- Ferrite 220 Ω → 600 Ω GZ1608D601TF (Basic); more filtering on 3V3_MIC, which draws only ~10 mA.
- +5V buck divider 100k/13.3k + 75 pF → 75k/10k + 100 pF (5.07 V, same feed-forward zero) and EN divider 511k/105k → 510k/100k (UVLO ≈ 7.3 V): all Basic parts, 3 fewer extended types.
- VDRV divider 150k/13.3k → 150k/13k (Basic): VDRV 15.4 V instead of 15.1 V, within the UCC27524 range.
- Inductor footprints follow the chosen parts: Sunlord SWPA6045S for L2–L4; L1 stays SRN8040 (Bourns SRN8040TA-100M, Isat 5 A, the TPS54302's typical current limit).
- One 0805 10 µF X7R moved to 1206 to share the part already used on 11 other lines.
- Mic bypass 100 nF C0G moved from 0805 to 1206: JLCPCB stocks no 100 nF C0G in 0805.
- Ceramic capacitors that only need X5R/X7R share one Basic part where the voltage allows (e.g. all 303 × 100 nF are CC0603KRX7R9BB104, 50 V).
